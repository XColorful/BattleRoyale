# 非对称竞技
不同阵营角色带来截然不同的游戏目标和体验

## 猎人游戏
在悬殊的装备差异下顽强拼搏

---

### 猎杀时刻
99玩家围剿1名猎物玩家。猎手仅能获得近战武器，并周期性获得一些关于猎物的提示。游戏期间地图随机生成高伤害封锁光柱，由于人数差距导致期望上会削减猎手状态（极小概率猎手直接胜利）。猎物需要想尽一切办法活到最后，游戏管理员发全自动大狙，开始最后的猎杀时刻，谁能笑到最后？

#### 游戏前准备
**游戏地图**：
- 任意选择原版地形或一张512x512至2048x2048或8192x8192的游戏地图

**游戏配置**：
- 物资刷新配置仅包含近战武器，管理员后期手动发放高伤害栓狙或终极猎手等反击手段
> 铠甲合体！

#### 建议规范
**游戏规则配置**：
- 游戏时长10-30分钟
- 根据“挖三填一”策略在地图中对猎物的强度选用冒险模式
- 队伍规模为99人，共2队
- 禁用自然恢复，启用自动饱和度

**出生配置**：
> 可选择猎物出生在中心而猎手出生点围成一圈，或者两阵营出生在固定/随机地点
> 若围成一圈，则猎手出生点应根据玩家数量平均分配4个方向（使得4/8/16时刚好平分）
> 固定地点可以是双方从地图两边界开始，猎手在中心而猎物在角落，猎手在角落而猎物在中心
- 围成一圈：猎物为队伍1（id小于猎手队），地图中心点坐标+围成一圈的出生点
- 两阵营各一个固定点位
- 纯随机出生在边界范围内

**区域配置**：
- 固定边界安全区：不透明颜色，秒杀伤害
- 缩圈安全区：**低透明颜色**，**高伤害**
- 随机封锁光柱：低透明颜色，高伤害

---

- 使用时间刷新词条控制后期刷新较弱于终极猎手初始装备的反击手段
> 基于物资刷新特性，终极猎手可以在刷新时间前跑遍全图以触发所有时间刷新词条，使无法刷新，而求生者可用人海战术逼迫猎手消耗时间以阻拦其进程

# English
Different faction characters bring completely different game goals and experiences.

## The Hunt
Persevere through vast equipment differences.

---

### The Killing Hour
99 players hunt down 1 prey player. Hunters can only obtain melee weapons and periodically receive hints about the prey. High-damage blockade beams randomly generate on the map during the game, which are expected to weaken hunters due to numerical disparity (very small chance for hunters to win directly). The prey must try every means to survive until the end, and the game administrator issues a full-auto heavy sniper, starting the final killing hour. Who will have the last laugh?

#### Pre-Game Setup
**Game Map**:
- Any vanilla terrain or a game map from 512x512 to 2048x2048 or 8192x8192.

**Game Configuration**:
- Loot spawner config only includes melee weapons, administrators manually issue high-damage bolt-action snipers or Ultimate Hunter counter-measures later.
> Armor up!.

#### Recommended Specifications
**Game Rules Configuration**:
- Game duration: 10-30 minutes.
- Use adventure mode for prey strength in the map based on "Dig Three Fill One" strategy.
- Team size: 99 players, 2 teams total.
- Disable natural regeneration, enable auto saturation.

**Spawn Configuration**:
> Choose whether prey spawns in the center and hunters spawn in a circle, or both factions spawn at fixed/random locations.
> If in a circle, hunter spawn points should be evenly distributed in 4 directions based on player count (so 4/8/16 are perfectly split).
> Fixed locations can be both factions starting from map boundaries, hunters in the center and prey in a corner, hunters in a corner and prey in the center.
- In a circle: Prey is Team 1 (ID less than hunter team), map center coordinates + circle spawn points.
- Each faction has one fixed spawn point.
- Purely random spawns within the boundary.

**Zone Configuration**:
- Fixed Boundary Safe Zone: Opaque color, instant kill damage.
- Shrinking Safe Zone: **Low transparency color**, **high damage**.
- Random Blockade Beams: Low transparency color, high damage.

---

### Reversal of Fortune
After surviving The Killing Hour, the prey, for their dream, transforms into the Ultimate Hunter, fighting fiercely like a whirlwind until the resilient survivors face the final twist. In the early game, one player is extremely powerful. In the late game, powerful weapons weaker than the Ultimate Hunter's initial equipment generate on the map for all survivors to use. Can the Ultimate Hunter still fight fiercely like a whirlwind?

#### Pre-Game Setup
**Game Map**:
- Any vanilla terrain or a game map from 512x512 to 2048x2048.

**Game Configuration**:
- Initial Ultimate Hunter's loot spawner includes powerful firearm weapons (for vanilla maps, replace with high-enchantment weapons).
- Use time entry to control later generations of counter-measures that are weaker than the Ultimate Hunter's initial equipment.
> Based on loot generation characteristics, the Ultimate Hunter can run across the entire map before generate time to trigger all time entries, preventing them from spawning. Survivors can use human wave tactics to force the hunter to consume time and hinder their progress.

#### Recommended Specifications
**Game Rules Configuration**:
- Game duration: 10-30 minutes.
- Use adventure mode for hunter strength in the map based on "Dig Three Fill One" strategy.
- Team size: 99 players, 2 teams total.
- Disable natural regeneration, enable auto saturation.

**Spawn Configuration**:
> Choose whether the Ultimate Hunter spawns in the center and survivors spawn in a circle, or both factions spawn at fixed/random locations.
> If in a circle, survivor spawn points should be evenly distributed in 4 directions based on player count (so 4/8/16 are perfectly split).
> Fixed locations can be both factions starting from map boundaries, survivors in the center and Ultimate Hunter in a corner, survivors in a corner and Ultimate Hunter in the center.

- In a circle: Ultimate Hunter is Team 1 (ID less than survivor team), map center coordinates + circle spawn points.
- Each faction has one fixed spawn point.
- Purely random spawns within the boundary.

**Zone Configuration**:
- Fixed Boundary Safe Zone: Opaque color, instant kill damage.
- Shrinking Safe Zone: **Low transparency color**, **high damage**.
